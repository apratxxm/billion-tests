The method

Dip. Scan. Interpret.

One-click operation. No clinical medical training.
Works at a home, a health camp, or a remote village.

1

Dip

Dip any government-approved strip in a fresh urine sample. Any time of day — first-morning sample not required.

2

Scan

Place it on the BTCardX™ and scan with a phone camera. Lighting, glare, angle & sensor variance auto-corrected.

3

Interpret

Vision-AI reads 10+ parameters in under 20 seconds and saves a tamper-proof, blockchain-verified report.

Every result is saved, so you can easily keep track of your test results over time — and identify trends or patterns that may be affecting your health.

the key innovation

The BTCardX™ converts your phone into a clinical-grade scanner.

A proprietary reference calibration card that dynamically compensates for camera differences, ambient lighting, strip angle and manufacturer — delivering 98%+ clinical accuracy with zero dedicated hardware.

Any camera Any lighting Any strip brand No expensive hardware No maintenance
