## The one-paragraph version

One-page marketing site for BillionTests, a urine-strip AI diagnostics platform. Single `index.html` file — Tailwind via CDN, no build step, no framework, no backend. Nine sections: hero, how-it-works, the BT Card innovation, the health-crisis stats, why-us comparison, non-invasive-vs-blood pitch, what-a-test-reveals, two deployment options, and a credibility/team block — followed by a combined demo-booking + partner-recruitment section and a footer. Real product photos and a real reference-card photo are in; a real demo video is sitting in the folder but not on the page. Contact emails, phone number, and a handful of team names/bios are live; several team members still have no photo, title, or contact info. The "download the app" QR codes on the page are decorative, not real — there's no app store link anywhere yet.
